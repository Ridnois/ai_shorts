# Style: brand, persona, words

Look, layout and motion: [[Design system]]. What on-screen text may say: [[Playbook]] §4.

## Brand

- **Inside the Model**, handle **@insidethemodelai** (claimed by the user in Studio). Banner line "How AI works · Agents at work · What's coming" and "NEW SHORTS MON TO FRI". **Mascot, the peeker:** the model as a dark box with its lid open, a round ember creature with big eyes peeking out, two signal tokens floating up; drawn in the look tokens by `System/Brand/Inside the Model/brand.py` (`itm-avatar.png` 800 px, `itm-banner.png` 2560 × 1440, text inside the 1546 × 423 safe area). `System/Brand/Redtail` is the retired fox brand, kept for its scripts only; TikTok still reads @redtail_media until the user renames it.
- **Mission:** teach people AI (how it works, how to make agents do real work, what it changes) in a minute they want to watch again and repeat to a friend.
- **Pillars:** precise (model, version and date on every claim) · useful (the viewer can do or explain something new after one minute) · calm (no hype, no doom) · honest about machines (AI voice, schematics and speed-ups disclosed) · real over decorative (real runs, real pages, no AI clichés).
- The brand lives on the channel page, **never inside the video**. The sign-off is an idea, never "like and subscribe".
- **Does not do:** clickbait, fake experts, staged or retyped demos, drawn mock-ups of real products, photoreal fakes of real events or people, copied scripts, template videos where only the topic word changes, launch-day news recaps.

## Persona: the engineer who uses it every day

Builds with models and agents daily and reads the papers behind the headlines; precise, quietly confident, dry, neither booster nor doomer. Specific beats general ("Vaswani and colleagues at Google, 2017", not "researchers"; "Claude Opus 5.5 in October 2026", not "AI"). The voice: calm and warm, like a curious specialist explaining something to a friend.

## Words

- **Banned** in narration, cards and descriptions: *did you know, game-changing, revolutionary, incredible, mind-blowing, insane, unlock, supercharge, journey, the takeaway, fun fact, key point, why it matters, in summary, in this video, this changes everything, AGI is here*, memes and exclamation marks.
- **Symbols:** never ≈ ≥ ≤ → in a card or callout; write "about", "or more", "to", "·".
- **Recurring devices:** the ember hero, one number that builds, part-name callouts, source labels on cards, the comment prompt, the last line.
