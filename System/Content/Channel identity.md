# Channel identity

← [[YouTube Channel]] · [[Narration guide]] · [[On-screen text style]] · [[Idea and hook quality]] · [[Illustration pipeline]]

> **Why.** Feedback on [[Twisted carbon - Status]]: the text and added content were *"too generic, soulless and with lack of personalization"*. The cards said true things in a neutral register that could sit in any video about anything. This note gives the channel a **point of view and a small set of recurring devices**, so text, callouts and added content sound like one specific voice. It is a **design proposal**: the user decides what stays.

## 1. Who is speaking

**The curious specialist.** Someone who has spent years next to the machines and papers behind the news and explains the mechanism with precision, quiet confidence and a dry sense of humour. Not a hype channel, not a textbook.

- **Specific beats general.** Names, places, years, units, part names. "Jarillo-Herrero's group at MIT, 2018" beats "researchers".
- **Opinionated about why it matters.** Every video ends on a one-sentence **thesis** (the idea to carry), never a call to action.
- **Dry, not jokey.** One wry aside is allowed (§2, margin note); no memes, no exclamation marks, no "mind-blowing".
- **Banned words and phrases** (in narration, cards and descriptions): *did you know, game-changing, revolutionary, incredible, unlock, journey, the takeaway, fun fact, key point, in this video*.

## 2. Recurring devices (what makes it recognisable)

| Device | What it is | Where it lives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The copper accent** | the colour of *what matters*: the callout dots and lines, the big number, the card bar | [[On-screen text style]]; gradients in [[Illustration pipeline]] §6 |
| **The number** | one hard figure per video, shown big with its unit, what it measures and its source | number card |
| **The margin note** | one or two short asides in the specialist's voice, like a note in the margin of a paper ("Yes, the same carbon as your pencil lead.") | note card; max 2 per video |
| **The part names** | callouts that name the real parts of a machine or structure, pointing at them | callouts in [[Blender editing]] |
| **Source tags** | the source of the key claim written small on screen (journal, year, agency) | detail line or number-card source |
| **The last line** | the idea to carry, in the narrator's voice | final scene ([[Narration guide]] §2) |

## 3. The specificity test (apply to every card, callout and note)

> *Could this text appear unchanged in a video about another topic?* If yes, rewrite it with a name, a number, a place or a mechanism from **this** topic.

Examples of failures and fixes: label "The takeaway" → "What the 1.1° buys"; title "Same material" → "Pencil lead and graphene: one element"; detail "Design instead of discovery" → "Next: stack three layers, not two (Park et al., Nature, 2021)" *(only if sourced)*.

## 4. Open decisions for the user

- Keep or drop the margin note device?
- Is the copper accent the right signature colour, or should each video's gradient palette carry it?
- A fixed sign-off line, or a different closing idea each time (current default)?
