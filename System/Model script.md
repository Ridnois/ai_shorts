## Annotated scenes

| Scene | Voice | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | "Copper carries current better than any metal but silver, and a passive cable draws no power. So one Nvidia rack uses over two miles of it." | One mechanism (why copper), joined by **so**; a detail a generic explainer skips (passive cable) |
| 3 | "But AI is not the only buyer. Buildings, power grids and cars take the biggest shares of the copper." | Breaks the viewer's assumption: the **turn**, in plain words |
| 5 | "That is why a new mine is slow. Someone must find the deposit, drill it to prove the ore, win permits and build. It averages seventeen years." | **Bridge** ("That is why") back to the question; one figure, spoken once |
| 7 | "So the limit on AI may be geology, not money or chips: copper must be found, dug and refined, and none of it can be rushed." | **Payoff**: answers the question, repeatable to a friend, hedged as inference, no moral |

Every scene opens by pointing at the previous one (so, but, that is why, meanwhile). Nothing is narrated ("and then"); each sentence is a cause or an effect.

## Hook rewrites (weak to strong)

Judged by the five-part hook test in [[Playbook]] §2, not by length. Examples, not verified facts beyond the notes they come from.

| Weak | Why weak | Stronger | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| "One AI rack holds over two miles of copper cable." | A number with no reason to care; the question it opens (why so much?) is not the video's question | "AI could run short of copper, and a new mine takes seventeen years." (used in r2) | Consequence plus a surprise; opens "why can't they just dig more?", which the video answers |
| "Electric car batteries lose about two percent a year." | A figure; nobody asks "why?" | "Your electric car's battery will probably outlast the car, unless you do one thing." | Stake for the viewer plus an open loop (what is the one thing?); only if the script then delivers it |
| "Your battery runs on a metal that floats." | Curious, but gives no stake and the answer is trivia | "The metal in your battery is so light it floats, and one country controls its supply." | Adds the stake; check the claim's wording against the research note first |

## Before you pick a hook

Say it aloud to a friend. If they say "so?", it fails the test. If they ask "why?" or "how?", keep it.
