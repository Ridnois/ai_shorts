# /short-script: the words, the package, the beats, the prediction

Rules: `System/Story.md` (whole), `System/Strategy.md` §Hooks and §Package, `System/Picture.md` §Medium and §Bar, `System/Data.md` §Before the upload.

## Write

1. **Research note first:** 6 to 12 facts with source and date, the one running example, what you could not verify. Every AI claim: model, version, date.
2. **Package:** the promise (one sentence the video must pay), the hook pattern, 3 titles (search words first, 40 to 60 characters), each set beside the 5 nearest `Research/Radar.md` outlier titles; frame-0 text (3 to 5 words); thumbnail idea.
3. **Script table**, one row per scene: scene · beat (hook, stake, mechanism, turn, payoff) · voice text · words · question it raises · what the viewer sees happen · source. Scenes become `plan.json` `texts`.
4. **Main picture and spine:** the medium of the mechanism ([[Picture]] §Medium), the one real run or model output, and the spine that returns between beats. Write the first idea, then two of other kinds; the first is usually the category's costume (a brain for "thinks"): pick the one only this topic could use, and say why in a line.
5. **Beat sheet**, one row per event: the cue word · importance 1, 2 or 3 · medium · the picture event · camera · transition · on-screen text · sound. Read 2 notes in `Research/Shots/` first and name the move borrowed from each.
6. **Pre-flight card** in Status ([[Data]] §Before the upload): pattern and evidence, title against the field, the first 7 seconds (0 s, 3 s, 6 s), the weakest beat, the prediction at 7 d.

## Self-test (write the results under the table; any fail is rewritten, not explained)

- [ ] 130 to 170 words (45 to 60 s at rate 1.1); count them
- [ ] Hook: 12 words or fewer, one named pattern, the subject the viewer knows, the search keyword, no banned opener; frame-0 text and picture say the same thing, on the end state
- [ ] **4 to 7 s rule:** sentences 2 and 3 raise the stakes; no date, paper, author, trial size or institution in the voice before 10 s (the source is on screen)
- [ ] The mechanism uses the one running example, countable on screen; each step changes the picture
- [ ] A turn ("but") at 50 to 65 % of the words; a re-hook every 8 to 12 s
- [ ] Payoff the viewer can use or repeat; the last line read into the first plays as one sentence; the last picture returns to frame 0 or lands a before → after number
- [ ] Ear: 6 to 16 words a sentence, joined by because, so, but; numbers and acronyms as spoken; no exclamation marks, no banned words or copy shapes ([[Story]] §Persona)
- [ ] Cut test run: list the sentences you deleted
- [ ] Every number, abstract noun and "but" has a picture in the beat sheet; at most a quarter of the beats are importance 3; one word is acted out
- [ ] Every number in the voice has a source and date in the table

## Fixing

When the user's feedback is about the words, change the script and rerun the self-test; when the feedback is a rule, edit `System/Story.md` per [[Data]] §Feedback loop. A changed hook re-checks the package and the pre-flight card.
