# /short-script: the words, the package, the beats

Rules: `System/Format.md` §3 (whole), `System/Look.md` §Brand (persona, banned words), `System/Growth.md` §Package.

## Write

1. **Research note first:** 6 to 12 facts with source and date, the one running example, what you could not verify. Every AI claim: model, version, date.
2. **Package:** the promise (one sentence the video must pay), 3 titles (search words first, 40 to 60 characters ideal), frame-0 text (3 to 5 words), thumbnail idea (one picture + 3 to 4 words).
3. **Script table**, one row per scene: scene · beat (hook, stake, mechanism, turn, payoff) · voice text · words · question it raises · what the viewer sees happen · source. Scenes become `plan.json` `texts`.
4. **Beat sheet**, one row per event: the cue word · the picture event · camera (wide, medium, close, move) · transition in · on-screen text · sound.

## Self-test (write the results under the table; any fail is rewritten, not explained)

- [ ] 130 to 170 words in total (45 to 60 s at rate 1.1); count them
- [ ] Hook: 12 words or fewer, the strangest true fact, names a subject the viewer knows, says the search keyword, no banned opener; the frame-0 text and picture say the same thing
- [ ] Stake by 10 s: a named cost or surprise with a number
- [ ] The mechanism uses the one running example; each step changes the picture
- [ ] A turn ("but") at 50 to 65 % of the words; a re-hook every 8 to 12 s
- [ ] Payoff the viewer can use or repeat; the last line read into the first plays as one sentence (the loop)
- [ ] Ear: sentences of 6 to 16 words, joined by because, so, but; numbers and acronyms as spoken; no exclamation marks, no banned words
- [ ] Cut test run: list the sentences you deleted
- [ ] Every number in the voice has a source and date in the table

## Fixing

When the user's feedback is about the words, change the script and rerun the self-test; if the feedback is a rule, edit `System/Format.md` per [[Feedback loop]]. A changed hook re-checks the package.
