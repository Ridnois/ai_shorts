### Key Takeaways

1. **Reach & Broad Appeal (Advantage: Materials Science & Physical Resources)**
    
    - Your videos focusing on tangible materials (lithium, copper, EV batteries, deep-sea minerals) drive **significantly more total viewership** (160–280 views each).
    - Topics about physical limits, resources, and tangible constraints tend to draw wider general curiosity because they feel grounded and real-world.
2. **Viewer Engagement & Retention (Advantage: Relatable Tech / Everyday Computing)**
    
    - [Why Your Phone Won't Screenshoot Netflix](https://studio.youtube.com/video/_lb5oDC7s64) achieved your highest initial hold rate among the higher-viewed topics (**52.6% stayed to watch**) and strong retention (**59.0%**).
    - Everyday, relatable tech questions grab immediate attention quickly, whereas more abstract computer science topics like data center hardware saw lower uptake.

### Strategic Sweet Spot: The Intersection

Your best-performing angle appears to be **bridging the two fields**—explaining how physical materials and engineering constrain modern computing and AI:

- [Why Copper Could Limit the AI Race](https://studio.youtube.com/video/YRX0VfpPMtU) and your latest upload on steel mill gases for silicon chips combine material science with high-interest tech topics.
- **Recommendation**: If choosing between them, **material science and real-world supply chain stories** are currently attracting more interest on your channel, especially when tied directly to everyday technologies viewers care about.
