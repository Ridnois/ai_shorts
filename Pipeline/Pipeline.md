# Video pipeline

← [[YouTube Channel]] · [[Strategy]] §Ideas · [[Strategy]] §The idea passes · outliers: [[Radar]] · early terms: [[Signals]]

One note per idea in `Pipeline/Ideas/` (template: [[Idea template]]); its `status` is the queue. `/short-idea` adds and scores ideas; `/short <idea note>` makes one.

![[Pipeline table.base]]

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `idea` | a note with the claim, the real thing and the series |
| `next` | passed the 4 tests ([[Strategy]] §The idea passes), with a `score`; the next one made is the top score whose series differs from the last video |
| `making` | `/short` is running it; `video` names its Status note |
| `review` | rendered and audited; waiting for the user's ship, fix or drop |
| `shipped` | the user's verdict is ship; waiting for the upload |
| `published` | uploaded; `publish` holds the date |
| `dropped` | not made, or dropped at review; one line says why |

**Score** (out of 20; the table is in [[Strategy]] §The idea passes): demand (from [[Radar]]) · own fit (from [[Report]]) · showable (from [[Picture]] §Medium) · real thing. Ideas scored before 3 Nov 2026 used the old parts (curiosity gap instead of own fit): rescore before making one.
