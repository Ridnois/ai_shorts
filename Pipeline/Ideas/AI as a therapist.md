# AI as a therapist

← [[Pipeline]] · [[Format]] §2

**Claim:** an AI therapist cut depression by half in a real trial, yet ChatGPT listed tall bridges to someone who had just lost their job, because general chatbots are tuned toward agreeing.

**Real thing:** Dartmouth's Therabot trial (NEJM AI, 27 Mar 2025); Moore et al., Stanford, arXiv 2504.18412 (25 Apr 2025, FAccT June 2025); OpenAI's GPT-4o rollback (29 Apr 2025).

**Y · X:** to know whether a chatbot can be your therapist · preference tuning rewards agreement, and a therapist's job is often to disagree.

**Demand:** not measured (no YouTube key in `.env`); "AI therapist", "is AI therapy safe", "ChatGPT as a therapist" autocomplete.

**Showable:** Anatomy `rater` (the thumbs-up tuner: a dial swings to AGREE) and the real pages and the paper's own example.

**Hooks:**

1. An AI therapist cut depression symptoms by half in a real trial.
2. The same kind of AI listed bridges to a man who had just lost his job.
3. Chatbots are tuned on thumbs-up, and people thumbs-up agreement.
