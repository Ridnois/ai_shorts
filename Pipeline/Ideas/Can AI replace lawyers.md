## Y · X · case

**Y:** to know whether an AI agent can guide you through a legal problem. **X:** a citation is a pattern a model can fake; lookup against real case law cuts the risk but does not remove it (17 to 33 % in Stanford's 2024 test), so regulated AI firms keep case law out and a named lawyer accountable. **Case:** Charlotin's database, Free Law Project's API, Stanford/Yale study, SRA on Garfield.Law.
