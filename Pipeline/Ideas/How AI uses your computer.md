# How AI uses your computer

← [[Pipeline]] · [[Strategy]] §The idea passes

**Claim:** an AI agent clicking around your screen is a loop of screenshots: it looks, guesses where to click, and looks again.

**Real thing:** Anthropic's computer use, public beta with the upgraded Claude 3.5 Sonnet (22 Oct 2024), 14.9 % on OSWorld screenshot-only vs 7.8 % next best; today's agents (OpenClaw, Claude) run the same loop. Plus a recorded run of our own (speed-ups marked).

**Y · X:** to know what an agent can and can't do on your machine · it sees pixels, not buttons; each step is a screenshot and a coordinate.

**Demand (31 Oct 2026):** youtu.be/NAauIR6JFps 5,990,755 (91.9x of 65,160); youtu.be/9DxXDLhXnwU 876,469 (3.8x of 229,419); youtu.be/zK38VMVV_Fs 437,064 (618.2x of 707); youtu.be/ROOKYw-fYU4 5,347 (12.6x).

**Showable:** our recorded run with each screenshot frozen and the click coordinate drawn, Anatomy camera-and-hand loop.

**Hooks:**
1. Strange fact: The AI controlling your computer is basically taking screenshots really fast.
2. Number: In 2024 the best agent finished 15 % of computer tasks.
3. Stakes: Give an agent your screen, and it sees everything on it.
