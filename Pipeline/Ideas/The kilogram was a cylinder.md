# The kilogram was a cylinder

← [[Pipeline]] · [[YouTube Channel]] · [[Idea and hook quality]]

## Idea

**Plain question:** why did the world stop defining the kilogram with one metal cylinder, and what defines it now?

**Theme line (rule 0.9):** *the kilogram stopped being an object because the object was changing.*

## Angle

Until 2019 a platinum-iridium cylinder made in **1889** *was* the kilogram, by definition. The deep why: **if the standard itself changes, nothing exists to say so**. Compared with its official copies it had diverged by about **50 micrograms** over a century, so no one could tell who was right. On **20 May 2019** the kilogram was tied to a constant of nature, the **Planck constant** (fixed at exactly 6.62607015×10⁻³⁴ J·s), and a **Kibble balance** turns electricity and motion into mass.

## Five candidate hooks (plain words, ≤ 10 words)

2. "Your bathroom scale traces back to one metal cylinder."
3. "The kilogram is now defined by a number, not an object."
4. "A metal cylinder and its copies slowly stopped agreeing."
5. "For 130 years, nobody could check the world's kilogram."

## Skeleton (7 scenes, ≤ 5 facts)

1 the cylinder (hook) → 2 so what that meant: by definition it was exactly one kilogram, copies sent to countries → 3 and that is the catch: copies and original drifted apart by about 50 micrograms, and there was no outside reference → 4 so they tied the kilogram to a constant of nature, the Planck constant, in 2019 → 5 how you weigh with a constant: the Kibble balance → 6 **payoff:** any lab can now make a kilogram, no vault needed.
