# Why AI drinks water

← [[Pipeline]] · [[Format]] §2

**Claim:** a chatbot answer costs water, and Google's data centres drank billions of gallons last year.

**Real thing:** Google's Environmental Report: about 7.2 billion gallons of freshwater consumed in 2024 (Axios on the 2025 report); 2025 figure in the 2026 report reported as 10.5 or 10.9 billion (sources disagree: read the primary PDF at P1). Per-answer cost: Li et al., *Making AI Less Thirsty* (arXiv 2304.03271, 2023) and Google's Gemini per-prompt disclosure (Aug 2025). Verify both at source.

**Y · X:** to know what the water headlines really mean · chips turn power into heat; evaporative cooling towers boil water away to remove it.

**Demand (youtube_search.py, 31 Oct 2026):** youtu.be/QGy2YOVWa4E 409,934 (302.9x of 1,353); youtu.be/_RPS7qpXgOk 57,366 (903.4x of 63); youtu.be/9xFhnSEY1vI 23,966,881 (7.9x of 3,040,323); youtu.be/BGNxvMtq8p4 36,562 (11.0x).

**Showable:** Anatomy cooling tower (chip heat → water loop → vapour plume), report page crops, a counter of gallons per day.

**Hooks:**
1. Strange fact: Your chatbot answer just evaporated a sip of water.
2. Number: Google's data centres drank about 7 billion gallons in one year.
3. Contradiction: Computers hate water. AI data centres can't run without it.
