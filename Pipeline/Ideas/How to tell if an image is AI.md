# How to tell if an image is AI

← [[Pipeline]] · [[Strategy]] §The idea passes

**Claim:** Google stamps an invisible watermark in every image its AI makes, and you can check for it; everything else has no stamp.

**Real thing:** Google SynthID and the SynthID Detector portal (announced at I/O, 20 May 2025; wider opening reported Oct 2026, verify on blog.google). C2PA Content Credentials as the other half.

**Y · X:** to check a suspicious image yourself · watermarks hide a pattern in pixels; they only exist if the maker added one.

**Demand (31 Oct 2026):** youtu.be/y8kwHsJD2V8 139,120 (3.9x of 35,757); second outlier still to confirm (the 'is this video ai' search was cut off; rerun at P1).

**Showable:** a recorded check of a Gemini image in the portal, Hairline pixel pattern, a non-Google image coming back blank.

**Hooks:**
1. Strange fact: There's a signature hidden in AI images. You can't see it.
2. Contradiction: The detector says 'not AI'. It still might be.
3. Stakes: Before you share that photo, here's the 10-second check.
