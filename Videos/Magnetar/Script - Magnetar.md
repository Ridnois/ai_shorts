# Script - Magnetar

← [[YouTube Channel]] · [[Guidelines for new scripts]] · [[Narration guide]]

**Question:** how can a star the size of a city be heard across the galaxy?
**Length:** 151 words (planned in words; duration appears after the voice exists).

## Script (voice, exact text)

| Scene        | Voice                                                                                                                                         | Words | On-screen (figures go here, not in the voice) |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 1 · Hook     | In 2004, a star the size of a city cracked, and Earth felt it from fifty thousand light-years away.                                           | 19    | `SGR 1806-20 · 27 Dec 2004`                   |
| 2 · Context  | It was a magnetar: a neutron star twenty kilometers wide, heavier than the Sun. A teaspoon of it weighs four billion tons.                | 22    | `≈ 20 km · 4 billion tons per teaspoon`                        |
| 3 · Field    | Its magnetic field is about a trillion times Earth's, and a hundred million times stronger than any magnet we can build.  | 21    | `≈ 1 trillion × Earth · 100 million × lab magnets`          |
| 4 · Cause    | That field twists inside the star, and the strain builds against its solid crust, so eventually the crust breaks. A starquake.                | 21    |                                               |
| 5 · Turn     | On December twenty-seventh, one quake lit up the sky: in a tenth of a second, more energy than the Sun gives off in a hundred thousand years. | 27    | `0.1 s = 100,000 years of Sun`                |
| 6 · Evidence | Satellites recorded the brightest burst ever seen from beyond our solar system, and it lasted barely a fifth of a second. | 21    |                                               |
| 7 · Close    | That light left before farming existed, and one cracked star the size of a city still lit up our satellites.                                  | 20    |                                               |

Total: 151 words (sum of the scene counts).

## Five candidate hooks

1. **(chosen)** "In 2004, a star the size of a city cracked, and Earth felt it from fifty thousand light-years away."
2. "A teaspoon of this star weighs four billion tons."
3. "Its magnetic field is a hundred million times stronger than any magnet we can build."
4. "A star's crust can crack like an earthquake, and the flash outshone the full Moon."
5. "The most magnetic thing in the universe is twenty kilometers wide."
