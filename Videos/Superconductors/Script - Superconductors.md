## Sources and what was not verified (as of 4 Oct 2026)

- Facts 1 to 17 come from Wikipedia articles and the EIA page quoted above, **not from primary papers**. Before publishing, confirm in the primary sources: Drozdov et al., *Nature* 2015 (H3S) and 2019 (LaH10); Dias retractions (*Nature* 2022, 2023); the gravimeter record (the Wikipedia sentence cites a station log, not a paper).
- "Pairs move as one quantum state, so nothing scatters them" is a one-line simplification of BCS theory (scattering needs enough energy to break a pair, the energy gap). The note keeps it because the voice cannot carry more; the pair drawing is a schematic, not to scale (real pairs are far larger than the atom spacing; **size not verified here**).
- "Grids could recover part of the five percent": the 5 % covers transmission and distribution, including transformers; superconducting cables would remove only the cable share. The script says "part".
- Photos of levitation: the species of superconductor is not stated on the Commons page; the plate is captioned only "superconductor".
