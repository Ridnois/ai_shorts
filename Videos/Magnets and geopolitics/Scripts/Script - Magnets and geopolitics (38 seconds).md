## Summary

> **Data as of:** 3 Oct 2026.
> **Duration:** 4 scenes, ≈ 77 words of narration. The chosen voice measures **36.9 s** of voice alone, plus 3 s of hum and silence at the start: ≈ 39.9 s. To close at 38 s, "Shipments fell" from scene 3 is cut (≈ 1.5 s). To confirm when editing.

**What is kept.** In 38 seconds only one blow fits: **a line that stops because of an invisible part, the reason and a question**. The silence at the start, the disc as a thread and the rhyme between the line that stops and the one that returns are kept. Everything else is sacrificed, including the 10 Nov 2026 date, so this version **does not expire** with that deadline.

**Scene rules**

- **`[MAGNET]`**: a flat disc about 3 cm in diameter and 5 mm thick; matte silver-grey with a faint nickel sheen, barely bevelled edges, no letters or marks.
- **`[HAND]`**: an adult hand with no face or identifiable features; skin with a slight callus on the thumb pad, short nails, no rings or watch.
- People only as hands, backs or silhouettes; no faces, real people or logos. Chicago is an **illustrated and declared reconstruction**.
- A single slow movement per shot. On-screen text only where indicated.

## Script

#### Script · 1 The silence of the line
*0:00–0:09*

**Sound:** 2 seconds of the continuous hum of an assembly line. Suddenly, everything cuts. One second of total silence and the voice enters.

**Shot:** an enormous industrial hall seen from the end of an assembly line, at eye level, with the line receding into the background. On it, a row of white SUV bodies with no wheels or doors, stopped one after another until they disappear. At the sides, yellow robotic arms frozen mid-gesture. An orange beacon on a pole, off. There is nobody. The polished floor reflects the ceiling's light tubes. Movement: a very slow advance down the centre of the aisle.

**Emotion:** the unnatural calm of something that always moves and no longer does. Held breath.

> En mayo de 2025, una línea de montaje en Chicago dejó de moverse. Faltaba un imán.
>
> *In May 2025, an assembly line in Chicago stopped moving. A magnet was missing.*

→ [[#Voice · scene 1]] · [[#Image · shot 1]] · [[#Sound · scene 1]]

#### Script · 2 The car from the inside
*0:09–0:17*

**Shot:** a white SUV seen from three-quarters on a plain black background. The body becomes transparent like glass and lets the skeleton show: axles, steering column, seats. In three places (the brakes of a wheel, the steering, a seat) a copper point of light the size of a grain of rice lights up, one at a time and half a second apart, pulsing once. At the last one, the camera stops and the point stays in the foreground, taking the shape of `[MAGNET]`. Movement: a slow orbit around the car, a quarter turn.

**Emotion:** quiet wonder at the invisible; a slight vertigo.

> Un disco del tamaño de una moneda. Está en los frenos, la dirección, los asientos. Nadie lo ve, hasta que falta.
>
> *A disc the size of a coin. It is in the brakes, the steering, the seats. Nobody sees it, until it is missing.*

→ [[#Voice · scene 2]] · [[#Image · shot 2]]

#### Script · 3 The jar and the tray
*0:17–0:28 · two shots of ~5 s*

**Shot 3a:** a glass jar on a light wooden table, beside a large window. It is full of fine grey powder, to a finger from the rim. Above it the text "94 %" appears on a single line. The window light marks the powder level on the glass. Movement: fixed shot, with a minimal zoom.

**Emotion:** the slow dread of seeing a number very close to the limit.

**Shot 3b:** a warehouse shelf. A black foam tray with twenty round holes the exact size of the disc, in four rows of five, all empty. `[HAND]` enters from above with fingers extended, searches one hole, the next, and stops. Movement: a slow zoom on the stopped hand.

**Emotion:** the small blow of looking for something that was always there and is not.

> China fabrica el 94 % de estos imanes. En abril de 2025 exigió licencia para exportar siete tierras raras. Los envíos cayeron.
>
> *China makes 94 % of these magnets. In April 2025 it demanded a license to export seven rare earths. Shipments fell.*

→ [[#Voice · scene 3]] · [[#Image · shot 3a]] · [[#Image · shot 3b]]

#### Script · 4 The fridge
*0:28–0:38 · two shots*

**Sound:** the first click of a chain tightening and a hum that slowly rises, the same as in scene 1.

**Shot 4a (~3 s):** the hall of scene 1, from the same point and with the same framing. Everything is the same and, suddenly, the orange beacon lights up and spins, tinting the walls orange. The first body advances a few centimetres. Fixed shot.

**Emotion:** relief, but with memory: now it is known what it cost.

**Shot 4b (~7 s):** a kitchen in the afternoon. A white fridge door; in the centre, a crayon drawing (a sun and a crooked house) held by a round black magnet. Side window light, yellow and warm; a tap running and a child's distant voice are heard. The camera slowly approaches the magnet and stays on it. Cut to black.

**Emotion:** everyday tenderness and a small chill: all this hangs from something we do not see.

> La línea volvió a moverse. Pero otra cadena toma décadas. ¿Qué otras cosas pequeñas ocupan hoy el mismo lugar?
>
> *The line moved again. But another chain takes decades. What other small things occupy the same place today?*

*Close: no added music and no "subscribe"; the kitchen sound cuts to black.*

### Channel style

Defined by me (the style was pending): **handmade plasticine miniature, stop-motion style**. It is clearly handmade and nothing photographic, which meets the content guide's rule of not generating images that look like photos of real events. It is added at the end of **all** image prompts:

> *Handmade stop-motion claymation miniature diorama. Matte plasticine surfaces with faint fingerprint textures, soft rounded forms, tilt-shift shallow depth of field, soft warm studio light. Muted desaturated palette of warm greys, off-white and dusty blue, with a single copper-orange accent color reserved for what matters. Clearly a handmade miniature, not a photograph, no text, no logos, no faces.*

- **Accent:** copper-orange, reserved for what matters (the point of light in scene 2, the beacon in 4a, the crayon sun in 4b).
- **Parameters:** 1 MP, PNG. The initial frames in `16:9`; the finals are generated **from the initial as input image** and with `match_input_image`, to keep the framing (the 2 final, `16:9`).
- **Lesson:** the model tends to tint any object near the accent with copper; the reference prompts carry "no copper".

- **Output:** `render/imanes-38s-r1.mp4` (1920×1080, 24 fps, H.264 + AAC, 38.8 s) and `render/imanes-38s.blend`, which can be opened in Blender to adjust by hand.
- **Voice:** the four v3 takes, trimmed to the entry and exit silence, with 3 s of initial silence (the hum and the pause of scene 1 will go there). In scene 3 **"Los envíos cayeron" is cut** (`CORTAR_ENVIOS = True` in the script) to get close to 38 s.
- **Image:** each shot is its initial frame with the final fading over it; it is not camera movement. Shot 4b ends in a hard cut, as the script asks.
- **Verification:** I reviewed three frames (at 5, 24 and 36 s): the images fill the frame and the fade works. **I could not hear or watch the whole video**, so the pace and the sync of the voice with each image are unchecked.
