# Script · Bike stems (60 seconds)

← [[Bike stems - Status]] · [[Research - Bike stems]] · [[Guidelines for new scripts]] · [[YouTube Channel]]

> **Language:** recorded in **Spanish** ("potencia" = stem) before the English-only decision. Each scene shows the recorded Spanish text and an English translation.
> The file keeps the name "40 seconds" so as not to break links; **the current script is 60 s** (v2, 3 Oct 2026: "make it 60 seconds").
> **Central question:** how did the part joining the handlebar to the fork change, and what did each generation gain and lose?
> **Idea that makes you think:** an improvement is always a trade-off.
> **Data as of:** 3 Oct 2026. Claims and sources in [[Research - Bike stems]].

## How [[Guidelines for new scripts]] was applied

## Script (recorded)

#### 1 · Hook · 24 words

> La potencia une manillar y horquilla, y decide qué tan lejos, alto y firme te sientes. ¿Qué ganó y qué perdió en cada cambio?
>
> *The stem joins handlebar and fork, and decides how far, how high and how firm you feel. What did it gain and what did it lose in each change?*

#### 2 · Quill · 32 words

> Primero fue un tubo dentro de la horquilla, que se abría con una cuña o un cono. Para subir el manillar bastaba aflojar un perno. Pero exigía una horquilla roscada y pesada.
>
> *First it was a tube inside the fork, which opened with a wedge or a cone. To raise the handlebar it was enough to loosen a bolt. But it demanded a threaded, heavy fork.*

#### 3 · Threadless · 35 words

> En 1990 llegó el Aheadset: la potencia abraza la horquilla por fuera. Pesa menos y se arma con una sola llave. A cambio, la altura ya no sube y baja fácil: se ajusta con espaciadores.
>
> *In 1990 the Aheadset arrived: the stem clamps the fork from the outside. It weighs less and is assembled with a single wrench. In return, the height no longer goes up and down easily: it is adjusted with spacers.*

#### 4 · Measures · 36 words

> Luego, las medidas: el manillar pasó de veinticinco a treinta y un milímetros, y a treinta y cinco en 2012, al ensancharse. Y el largo bajó: en montaña, de más de cien a menos de sesenta.
>
> *Then, the measurements: the handlebar went from twenty-five to thirty-one millimetres, and to thirty-five in 2012, as it widened. And the length dropped: in mountain biking, from over a hundred to under sixty.*

#### 5 · Close · 32 words

> Hoy, en ruta, manillar y potencia ya son una sola pieza de carbono: según las marcas, más rígida y aerodinámica, pero casi sin ajuste. Cada mejora ganó algo y costó otra cosa.
>
> *Today, on the road, handlebar and stem are already a single carbon piece: according to the brands, stiffer and more aerodynamic, but almost without adjustment. Each improvement gained something and cost something else.*

**Total: 159 words ≈ 843 characters.** The real length is measured with the generated voice.
