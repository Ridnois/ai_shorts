# Research: how to make a lemon cake

← [[Lemon cake - Status]] · [[Script - Lemon cake (80 seconds)]] · [[YouTube Channel]] · [[Fast workflow]]

> **Central question:** which steps decide whether a cake comes out fluffy or hard, and why?
> **Clarification:** it is a recipe, not a news fact. What matters is that **the quantities and the why of each step are supported**. The sources **do not agree** on several figures (see below): the script uses a base recipe and says so. (The sources are Chilean Spanish-language pages; the dish is a *queque*, a loaf cake.)

## Summary

A *queque* (sponge cake, *keke*) is a batter of butter, sugar, eggs, flour, milk and baking powder. The steps that weigh most are six: ingredients at room temperature, beating butter with sugar, eggs one at a time, mixing the dry ingredients carefully, baking at 180 °C and checking with a toothpick.

## What gate P2 decides

- **My biggest risk:** I did not test it. Before publishing it is advisable to **bake it once** with these quantities; without that, the script is called a "base recipe from several sources".
- **Claim 13** is softened in the script (from "develops gluten, and it comes out hard" to "can come out hard").
