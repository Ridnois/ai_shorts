# Research: why helium cannot be made

← [[Helium - why it cannot be made - Status]] · [[Script - Helium - why it cannot be made (70 seconds)]] · [[YouTube Channel]] · [[Fast workflow]]

> **Central question:** why can't we just make more helium, and what does that change?
> **Data as of:** 3 Oct 2026. Earlier research on the 2026 supply shock is in [[Research - Helium]] and is reused where noted.

## Summary

Helium forms underground, one atom at a time, when uranium and thorium decay. Some gets trapped under rock with natural gas in a few places. It is so light that, once released, it escapes the planet. Nothing else gets as cold as liquid helium, so MRI magnets (and other cryogenic uses) cannot swap it out. Because it cannot be manufactured, the only lever left is to use less: new sealed MRI magnets hold 7 liters or even 0.7 instead of up to 1,500.
