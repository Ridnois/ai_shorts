# Research: countries, brands and Canon toward 2 nm

← [[Chips and lithography - Status]] · [[Script - Chips and lithography (85 seconds)]] · [[YouTube Channel]] · [[Fast workflow]]

> **Central question:** can a machine that stamps chips, instead of projecting them with light, break the dependence on ASML and TSMC for the most advanced chips?
> **User's focus (respected):** (1) how countries and brands compete in making chips; (2) Canon's progress toward 2 nm. **Data as of:** 3 Oct 2026.

## Summary

The most advanced chips are drawn with EUV lithography, and **only ASML** builds those machines. That concentrates manufacturing (TSMC) and turns the machines into a geopolitical weapon (export controls on China, subsidies in Japan and the US). Canon offers another technique, **nanoimprint (NIL)**: it presses a mold onto resin. Canon says that today it reaches the 5 nm level and could reach 2 nm with better molds; **2 nm is not achieved** and defects remain the obstacle.
