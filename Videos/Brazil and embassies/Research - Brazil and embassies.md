# Research: Brazil, elections and embassies

← [[Brazil and embassies - Status]] · [[Script - Brazil and embassies (60 seconds)]] · [[YouTube Channel]] · [[Fast workflow]]

> **Central question (v2):** in Brazil's first round, what weighs on the candidates and who pressures the vote? Embassies are one piece of that pressure.
> **Data as of:** 3 Oct 2026, one day before the vote. The situation changes by the hour: **re-check everything before recording**.
> **Clarification of the request:** Brazilian embassies were not closed. **Itamaraty warned it could close some from November** if it does not receive money. The closures actually carried out are those of the **US and Australia, in Brazil**. The script tells both things and does not mix them.

## Summary in five lines

1. The first round is Sunday 4 Oct 2026 (first Sunday of October, according to Brazilian electoral law; see claim 1).
2. On 2 Oct, the US suspended consular services at its embassy and consulates in Brazil, and Australia closed its embassy in Brasília, citing "security". The Federal Police found no threat.
3. The PT called it "unprecedented"; the Brazilian government saw no justification. Other embassies stayed open.
4. In parallel, Brazil's foreign ministry asked for R$ 352 million extra: its budget covers only until the end of October and, without it, it could close specific missions from November.
5. There are already effects: since 30 Jul 2026 passport issuance abroad has been reduced for lack of resources and record demand.

**The pattern behind the final question.** Of the three cases: one ended in a **final conviction** (Jair), one in **annulment for competence and partiality** (Lula) and one in **dismissal because evidence was annulled** (Flávio, 2022), and Flávio also has a new investigation. In two of the three, **nobody got to judge the facts**. That is verifiable. What I do **not** know, and the script does not claim, is what happened in the facts.

**On the "suspected coercion to sabotage the vote":** there are supported facts (20 and 21: armed coercion in neighbourhoods and vote buying) and an **attributed** suspicion (5: the PT reads the US closure as pressure). **I found no evidence of organised sabotage for or against a candidate**, and the script does not claim it.

## What gate P2 decides

- **The claims on the "Brazil closes" side (9 and 10) rest on weak sources.** Before recording, the original outlet (probably a national newspaper) or an official statement must be located. Until then the script uses "according to a memo released by the press" and the verb "could".
- **The election date** (claim 1) must be confirmed at the TSE.
- What is **not said**: why the US closed (only "for security", as it said) or whether there is interference: there is no solid source.
- Neutrality: the script informs and takes no side for Lula, for Bolsonaro or for the US.
