## 1. Idea gate

Chosen thread: **"the middle of the chain"**: each link of the AI power chain (mine, refinery, transformer, steel) is concentrated in a few places, and the middle links are the least visible. Scores: surprise 4 · scale 4 · depth 4 · evidence 4 · showable 5 · plain 4. Rejected, one line each: **the full four-act geopolitics** (nuclear, geothermal and North-South dynamics need sources I did not read); **a second "copper takes 17 years" video** (the Copper video); **a second "transformer wait" video** (the Transformer video); **a "who wins" ranking of nations** (speculation). **Street-lamp test: pass:** "AI needs power equipment" is a headline; the video adds the one number nobody frames (China refines 48 % of copper while mining 8 %) and lines up the other links with it. **Coffee test:** *"The AI buildout leans on copper, and almost half of the refined copper comes from one country that mines less than a tenth of it."* "Huh, the mines are somewhere else?": surprise 4. **Expert test:** each scene has a detail a generic explainer skips (mine versus refine, three countries dig half, 80 % imported, one steel maker, the IEA Japan comparison). **Against the idea:** (1) "this repeats Copper and Transformers" → new synthesis (shares to scale, the mine-versus-refinery gap) and none of the earlier headline numbers; (2) "China bad" framing → the voice only states shares; (3) "AI race winners" is speculation → hedged ("may be a refinery or a transformer factory").

**Demand check:** the user wrote the idea; topic is the channel's strongest family (Copper has the most views so far). **Not checked:** autocomplete, comparable Shorts views. **Topic family:** supply chains and energy infrastructure (after ocean mining).
