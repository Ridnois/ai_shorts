## 1. Idea gate

Chosen thread: **time, scale, the unfinished rulebook, the national shortcut.** Scores: surprise 4 · scale 5 · depth 4 · evidence 4 · showable 5 · plain 4. Rejected, one line each: **the full five-beat geopolitics** (China's contracts and US non-ratification of the sea-law treaty need sources I did not read: too many facts); **the environmental standoff alone** (a debate, not a mechanism); **a "who will win" angle** (speculation). **Street-lamp test: pass:** "deep-sea mining is controversial" is a headline; the video adds how the rocks grow (a few millimeters per million years), and that the international rulebook is unfinished while a national route is open. **Coffee test:** *"There are potato-sized rocks on the seafloor that took millions of years to grow, and there are no international rules yet for taking them."* "Wait, millions of years?": surprise 4. **Expert test:** each scene has a detail a generic explainer skips (a shark tooth as the seed, growth per million years, 21 billion tons, a rulebook that slipped again in July 2026, a second legal route through NOAA). **Against the idea:** (1) "it's a news story that will date" → the voice avoids dates; cards carry them; (2) "environmental activism" → no verdict, only what is unfinished; (3) "the idea note's nickel claim" → not used (fact 17).

**Demand check:** the user wrote the idea; it is current news (NOAA hearing 13 Oct 2026, ISA July 2026). **Not checked:** autocomplete, comparable Shorts views. **Topic family:** ocean and geopolitics (after energy infrastructure, semiconductors, history).
