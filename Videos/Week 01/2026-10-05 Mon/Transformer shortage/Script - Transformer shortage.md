← [[Transformer shortage - Status]] · [[YouTube Channel]] · [[Playbook]] · [[Model script]] · [[Youtube suggestions based on our videos]]

User request (5 Oct 2026): *"now make a video of the second place idea, be creative"*. **Second place = the second idea in the suggestions note, "The Silent Grid Crisis Threatening To Stall The Entire AI Boom"**, reframed around one object. **Creative angle:** a machine with **no moving parts** that still takes about two and a half years to get, shown as a *waiting line* (bars to scale), plus the one-maker steel. **Theme line:** *The grid part AI waits for is a steel ring wrapped in copper, and it cannot be built fast.* **One question:** why can't the AI boom just buy more transformers? **Length:** 139 words, about 50 s.

## 1. Idea gate

Chosen thread: **the transformer as an object** (what it is, why it waits, why it can't be hurried). Scores: surprise 4 · scale 4 · depth 4 · evidence 4 · showable 4 · plain 4 (surprise: "no moving parts, yet years"). Rejected: **the grid as a whole** (headline, vague), **GOES only** (one plate of facts, narrower), **the age of the fleet** (a different story; data is about distribution transformers). **Street-lamp test: pass:** "grid constraints slow AI" is a headline, the video adds what the object is, the real wait (128 weeks), and the one-maker steel. **Coffee test:** *"AI data centers wait about two and a half years for a steel ring wrapped in copper, and only one U.S. company makes the steel."* Friend: "wait, that's what's holding it up?" surprise 4. **Expert test:** each scene has a detail a generic explainer skips (no moving parts, 128 weeks, imports 80 %, GOES, 2028). **Against the idea:** (1) "I've heard the grid story" → this one is about one object; (2) a contractor says there is no shortage → caveat in the voice ("average", "may"); (3) shortage numbers are about 2025 → the voice says "now" only for the 128-week average and the Status/Upload notes the date.

**Demand check:** Studio rated it "very likely" (the suggestion note, tied with the water idea); the copper video, the closest cousin, is the best performer so far. **Not checked:** autocomplete and comparable Shorts views. **Topic family:** energy infrastructure (after semiconductors and materials).
