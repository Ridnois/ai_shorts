## 3. Five candidate hooks

1. **(chosen)** "Twist one atom-thin sheet of carbon one point one degrees over another, and it can carry electricity with zero resistance." *A reason (a strange fact) and its meaning; opens "why does a twist matter?".*
2. "A pencil lead and sticky tape won a Nobel Prize." Charming but a street lamp: a reaction, not a reason.
3. "Electrons in graphene move at one three-hundredth of the speed of light and behave as if they weigh nothing." Strong, but opens no thread toward superconductors or light.
4. "In 1999, light was slowed to the speed of a car." Strong visual hook, but the thread would be slow light, which only half of the topics support.
5. "Diamond and pencil lead are the same element. A one-degree twist makes it superconduct." Close to #1, longer.
