> **Thread (chosen by scoring, [[Research - Twisted carbon]]):** geometry is a material: a one-degree twist turns carbon into a superconductor, and metamaterials do the same for light.

## Rigor notes

- "Can carry electricity with zero resistance" is conditional: twisted bilayer graphene shows zero-resistance states **below ≈ 1.7 K, on doping**; the script says "can" and states the temperature in scene 4.
- Onnes cooled **mercury with liquid helium**; Tc of mercury ≈ 4.2 K, so 1.7 K is "even colder".
- "A European project … terahertz … slowing them" is the CORDIS description of GRAB-A-META (projected devices, 2015 to 2017); nothing is claimed about products.
- Trim if long: scene 5's last sentence.
