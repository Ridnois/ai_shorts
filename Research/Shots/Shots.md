**What the six share** (3 Nov 2026), now in [[Picture]] §Bar:

1. **Frame 0 shows the end state or the event mid-action** (5 of 6; 3Blue1Brown opens nearly empty and lets the spoken puzzle hook).
2. **One spine** (a chart, a map, a scorecard, a master diagram) **returns between beats** with the current part marked, so progress is visible.
3. **Every claim gets a literal picture on its word,** each held 1 to 3 s; text on screen is only the term or the number.
4. **The ending returns to the opening picture or lands a before → after number.**
