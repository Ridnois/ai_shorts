# Instagram: the watchlist and the niche

← [[Strategy]] §Package (how a Reel is posted) · [[Data]] §Instagram (its numbers) · [[Channels]] (the YouTube watchlist)

## What the niche looks like (3 Nov 2026)

- **The AI niche on Instagram is mostly tools, news and business.** Of the 25 accounts in one 2026 list (fixieit.com) and 20 in Favikon's AI educators list, most teach tool tips, automations or "make money with AI"; few explain how AI works. Same gap as on YouTube ([[Strategy]] §Audience and promise): our lane is open here too.
- **Most big AI accounts are a face on camera.** The ones that prove a faceless, animated explainer works on Instagram are 3Blue1Brown, ByteByteGo, Kurzgesagt and Zack D. Films (table below). We stay faceless; the paper look is the brand.
- **Closest "how AI works" voices:** Harper Carroll ("AI is less scary under the hood", ex-Stanford, ex-Meta) and Tiffany Janzen. Both talk to camera; the mechanism shown as a picture is ours.

## Finding content (by hand, about 20 minutes a week)

1. Logged in, open each watchlist account's **Reels** tab. Instagram shows a play count on each tile: note any AI Reel at **3x or more** of the account's typical tile in the table below, with its hook pattern ([[Strategy]] §Hooks) and why people clicked. Never copy words or looks.
2. Search Instagram for the viewer's plain question ("how does ChatGPT work", "why AI hallucinates"): the top Reels are demand, the gaps are ours.
3. A question that wins on both Instagram and the [[Radar]] scores **Demand 5** ([[Strategy]] §The idea passes). An Instagram outlier alone counts as one outlier.
